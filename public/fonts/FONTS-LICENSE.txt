The graffiti-*.woff2 files are letter subsets of these fonts, used by the
Graffiti Wall backdrop. All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font
License, Version 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org

- Rubik Bubbles (graffiti-rubik-bubbles.woff2)
- Sedgwick Ave Display (graffiti-sedgwick-ave-display.woff2)
- Permanent Marker (graffiti-permanent-marker.woff2)

Downloaded from Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com), where each font's
page lists its copyright holders.
