Privacy Policy

Stockism - A Lookism Stock Market Simulation Game

TL;DR: We store almost nothing about you. No real names, no profile pictures, no tracking.

What We Store in Our Game Database

  • Username - The name YOU choose (not your Google name)
  • Game data - Your cash balance, stock holdings, and trade history
  • Account ID - A random ID to identify your account
  • Discord ID - If you link Discord, so one Discord account maps to one player. Never shown to other players
  • IP address - Used only to stop people making alt accounts. Never shown to other players

What Firebase Authentication Stores

Firebase (Google's service) handles login and stores your email to manage your account. This is standard for any website with login - it's how you can sign back in later.

  • Email - Stored by Firebase Auth (not our game database). Never visible to other players or used for marketing.

What We DON'T Store Anywhere

  • Your real name - We never save the name from your sign-in account
  • Your profile picture - We never save your sign-in photo
  • Your password - Your sign-in provider handles authentication securely
  • Your contacts or account data - We have no access
  • Tracking cookies or analytics - We don't use any. When something breaks, an error report goes to our bug tracker so we can fix it

About the Sign-In Popup

When you sign in with Google, X, or Discord, they show a standard message saying we "could" access your name and profile picture. This is their default screen - it shows the maximum possible permissions, not what we actually use.

In reality, our code immediately discards this information. We only use the sign-in to verify you're a real person, then we ask you to create a username. That username is the only identifier visible to other players.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data anytime from within the app. Click your username to open your Profile, scroll to the bottom, and click "Delete Account". To stop alt-account abuse, a record of your IP address and Discord ID is kept after deletion, which blocks them from making a new account for 30 days.

Third-Party Services

Stockism uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication - For secure login (Google, X, Discord, Email)
  • Firebase Firestore - For storing game data
  • Vercel - For serving the website
  • Discord - For optional Discord login and account linking
  • Sentry - For error reports when something breaks
  • reCAPTCHA - For bot protection

The Firebase and reCAPTCHA services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Stockism is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
